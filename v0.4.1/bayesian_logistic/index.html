Logistic inference with RxInfer

The optional logistic backend approximates a Bernoulli-logit regression with an explicit Gaussian coefficient prior. GraphPPL specifies Gaussian subproblems, which RxInfer solves inside Pólya–Gamma/Jaakkola updates. The coefficient block retains its full covariance; factorisation separates it from the auxiliary variables. This is approximate logistic inference.

using CausalTargeted, RxInfer, GraphPPL, DataFrames, LinearAlgebra, Random
data = DataFrame(A = repeat([-0.7, -0.3, 0.3, 0.7], 8),
    Y = repeat([0, 1, 0, 1], 8))
prior = GaussianCoefficientPrior(mean = zeros(2),
    covariance = Matrix{Float64}(I, 2, 2))
fit = fit_bayesian_logistic(data, :Y, :A, Symbol[]; prior)
effect = bayesian_policy_contrast(fit, data,
    smooth_bounded_logit_shift_policy(-1.0, 1.0);
    deltas = [0.0, 0.5], n_draws = 2000, rng = Xoshiro(42))

Covariance and convergence

The default covariance=:linear_response differentiates the converged variational fixed point. Both variational_covariance and corrected_covariance remain available. The latter is a posterior covariance approximation, not a corrected joint posterior density. In particular, the correction vanishes at a zero-mean symmetric solution; it cannot repair every error in variational uncertainty.

Convergence checks coefficient means, covariance and second moments. Diagnostics also check response conditioning, stability, solve residuals and positive covariance. Failed checks withhold uncertainty: posterior_covariance and policy limits are NaN, while a labelled plug-in effect remains available. covariance=:variational explicitly selects raw moments. It is never silently substituted after a correction fails. Inspect fit.status, fit.diagnostics and effect.metadata.uncertainty. :converged denotes numerical convergence, not certified posterior accuracy.

Policy effects are nonlinear risk contrasts. A shared set of joint normal coefficient draws is pushed through the fixed target cohort and policy designs. With corrected covariance, this normal moment approximation is an additional approximation. Results include paired grid covariance, pointwise quantiles and Monte Carlo standard errors of the means. Increase n_draws to assess simulation stability; the exact Gaussian release's Student-t formulas do not apply here.

Assumptions and validation

Graph identification, model adequacy, basis provenance and MAR assumptions retain separate metadata. Priors never use outcome-derived scales. Missing predictors and hard-clamped policies remain unsupported. A proper prior keeps separated binary regressions computable without guaranteeing accurate posterior tails. Discrete policies require factual and assigned treatment levels to occur in the fitting data, including numeric levels. This check does not establish conditional positivity within covariate strata.

The independently reviewed derivation and numerical gates are in the logistic contract. The package tests compare Gaussian subproblems with analytic algebra, variational updates with an independent implementation, and response columns with fully reconverged tilted fits. Optional reference scripts compare posterior moments with converged low-dimensional quadrature and separate prior-predictive calibration from fixed-parameter coverage. Independent adaptive integration checks scalar tails and two deterministic nonlinear bivariate policy cases; unchecked grid-tail results remain exploratory. No universal calibration or improvement is claimed. See the validation report, which retains mean errors under separation and the zero-mean correction limit.

Sources: Durante and Rigon, Giordano, Broderick and Jordan.

CausalTargeted.GaussianCoefficientPrior — Type
GaussianCoefficientPrior(; mean, covariance)

An explicit multivariate normal prior on logistic regression coefficients, independent of the outcome. The finite, symmetric covariance must be positive definite; coefficients follow the frozen design column order.

CausalTargeted.BayesianLogisticFit — Type

Variational logistic coefficient moments, a frozen design, and explicit assumptions.

variational_covariance is the raw approximation. corrected_covariance stores linear-response moments when available. posterior_covariance contains the requested usable moments, or NaNs when convergence or correction fails. These moments do not define a corrected posterior density.

CausalTargeted.fit_bayesian_logistic — Function
fit_bayesian_logistic(data, outcome, treatment, covariates; prior,
    basis=nothing, identification=nothing, handle_missing=:error,
    maxiter=200, tol=1e-8, covariance=:linear_response)

Fit Bernoulli logistic regression with the optional RxInfer extension; load it with using RxInfer, GraphPPL. A proper coefficient prior must be specified. The default design and categorical levels are fitted once. A custom basis(predictor_data, treatment_values) receives only declared predictors and must preserve its column meanings. Missing predictors are rejected. Missing outcomes require explicit handle_missing=:mar; only observed outcomes enter the likelihood, under user-assumed ignorable MAR. With no observed outcomes, the coefficient posterior remains the specified prior.

Graph identification and outcome-model assumptions are recorded separately. A certificate does not certify a custom basis or regression adequacy. Neither variational moments nor linear-response corrections are exact posterior inference. Failed convergence or invalid requested correction withholds usable coefficient covariance; raw covariance is never silently substituted.
