# Hierarchical Bayesian Inference Contract

This document records the mathematical and software contract for hierarchical
Bayesian Gaussian inference in `CausalTargeted.jl`, prior to and alongside
implementation (see `plans/cluster-effects-bayesian-inference.md` in the CDCS
repository).

## 1. Three distinct roles of clustering

- **Sampling dependence**: observations within a cluster share cluster-level
  variation. Modelled explicitly by random effects; existing cluster-robust
  frequentist estimators remain separate.
- **Partial pooling**: cluster parameters borrow strength through shared
  hyperparameters and proper regularising priors.
- **Causal interference**: one unit's mediator or treatment affecting another
  unit's outcome is represented by explicit mechanism terms and intervention
  definitions, not merely by adding a random intercept.

A random intercept does not establish interference, and partial pooling does not
establish causal identification.

## 2. Hierarchical Gaussian model and prior contract

For clusters $k = 1, \dots, K$ with sizes $N_k$ ($N = \sum_{k=1}^K N_k$):

$$
y_k = X_k \beta + \mathbf{1}_{N_k} b_k + \varepsilon_k,
\qquad
b_k \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_b^2),
\qquad
\varepsilon_k \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_\varepsilon^2 I_{N_k}).
$$

Priors follow the `ConjugateGaussianPrior` convention for global coefficients
and residual variance, extended with a proper inverse-gamma prior on the cluster
variance (`ClusterGaussianPrior`):

$$
\beta \mid \sigma_\varepsilon^2 \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu_0, \sigma_\varepsilon^2 V_0),
\qquad
\sigma_\varepsilon^2 \sim \operatorname{InvGamma}(a_\varepsilon, b_\varepsilon),
\qquad
\sigma_b^2 \sim \operatorname{InvGamma}(a_b, b_b),
$$

with symmetric positive-definite $V_0$, $a_\varepsilon > 1$, $b_\varepsilon > 0$,
$a_b > 1$, and $b_b > 0$ so marginal prior variances exist. Prior locations,
scales, and coefficient ordering must be supplied explicitly; no outcome-based
"weakly informative" rescaling is performed.

## 3. Exact affine reparameterisation (frozen design)

Numerical standardisation of the design matrix is represented as an invertible
linear map $X_{\text{std}} = X A$ (centring non-intercept columns against the
intercept column when present, and scaling non-constant columns by their
training standard deviation). Setting $\beta = A \tilde{\beta}$ preserves the
exact model:

$$
X \beta = X_{\text{std}} \tilde{\beta},
\qquad
\tilde{\mu}_0 = A^{-1} \mu_0,
\qquad
\tilde{V}_0 = A^{-1} V_0 A^{-\top}.
$$

Posterior moments map back to the original coordinate system without
approximation:

$$
m_\beta = A m_{\tilde{\beta}},
\qquad
\Sigma_\beta = A \Sigma_{\tilde{\beta}} A^\top,
\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(\beta, b_k) = A \operatorname{Cov}(\tilde{\beta}, b_k).
$$

## 4. Exact Gaussian block algebra (Schur-complement elimination)

Conditional on precisions $\tau_\varepsilon = \sigma_\varepsilon^{-2} > 0$ and
$\tau_b = \sigma_b^{-2} \in (0, \infty]$ (where $\tau_b = \infty$ represents
the exact $\sigma_b^2 = 0$ non-hierarchical limit), the joint Gaussian precision
for $(\beta, b_1, \dots, b_K)$ is block-arrow:

$$
Q_{\beta\beta} = \tau_\varepsilon \bigl(V_0^{-1} + X^\top X\bigr),
\qquad
Q_{\beta b_k} = \tau_\varepsilon s_{X,k},
\qquad
Q_{b_k b_k} = d_k := \tau_\varepsilon N_k + \tau_b,
$$

where $s_{X,k} = X_k^\top \mathbf{1}_{N_k}$ and $s_{y,k} = \mathbf{1}_{N_k}^\top y_k$
are cached cluster sufficient statistics. No dense $N \times N$ or $K \times K$
matrix is allocated.

Eliminating $b_1, \dots, b_K$ yields the $p \times p$ Schur-complement system:

$$
\Lambda_\beta = Q_{\beta\beta} - \sum_{k=1}^K \frac{\tau_\varepsilon^2}{d_k} s_{X,k} s_{X,k}^\top,
\qquad
h_\beta = \tau_\varepsilon \left(V_0^{-1}\mu_0 + X^\top y - \sum_{k=1}^K \frac{\tau_\varepsilon s_{y,k}}{d_k} s_{X,k}\right),
$$

with $1/d_k := 0$ when $\tau_b = \infty$. Because $V_0 \succ 0$ and the
marginalised likelihood precision is positive semidefinite, $\Lambda_\beta \succ 0$
even for rank-deficient $X$ or prior-only ($N = 0$) inputs.

From the Cholesky factorisation of $\Lambda_\beta$:

- Global moments: $\Sigma_\beta = \Lambda_\beta^{-1}$, $m_\beta = \Lambda_\beta^{-1} h_\beta$.
- Conditional cluster law given $\beta$:
  $$b_k \mid \beta \sim \mathcal{N}\!\left(\frac{\tau_\varepsilon (s_{y,k} - s_{X,k}^\top \beta)}{d_k},\; \frac{1}{d_k}\right).$$
- Marginal cluster moments and cross-covariances:
  $$m_{b_k} = \frac{\tau_\varepsilon (s_{y,k} - s_{X,k}^\top m_\beta)}{d_k},
  \qquad
  \operatorname{Cov}(\beta, b_k) = -\frac{\tau_\varepsilon}{d_k} \Sigma_\beta s_{X,k},
  \qquad
  \operatorname{Var}(b_k) = \frac{1}{d_k} + \left(\frac{\tau_\varepsilon}{d_k}\right)^2 s_{X,k}^\top \Sigma_\beta s_{X,k}.$$
- Joint draws $(\beta^{(s)}, b^{(s)})$ are generated by drawing
  $\beta^{(s)} \sim \mathcal{N}(m_\beta, \Sigma_\beta)$ and then independently
  drawing $b_k^{(s)} \mid \beta^{(s)}$ for each cluster $k$.
- Expected sufficient sums of squares for structured VI updates:
  $$\mathbb{E}[\|b\|^2] = \sum_{k=1}^K \bigl(m_{b_k}^2 + \operatorname{Var}(b_k)\bigr),$$
  $$\mathbb{E}[\|y - X\beta - Z b\|^2] = y^\top y - 2 m_\beta^\top (X^\top y) + m_\beta^\top (X^\top X) m_\beta + \operatorname{tr}\bigl((X^\top X)\Sigma_\beta\bigr)$$
  $$+ \sum_{k=1}^K \Bigl(-2 m_{b_k} s_{y,k} + N_k (m_{b_k}^2 + \operatorname{Var}(b_k)) + 2 s_{X,k}^\top \bigl(m_\beta m_{b_k} + \operatorname{Cov}(\beta, b_k)\bigr)\Bigr).$$

## 5. Planned inference, refinement, and failure rules (Milestones 2–6)

- **Structured VI**: $q(\beta, b)\,q(\sigma_\varepsilon^{-2})\,q(\sigma_b^{-2})$,
  preserving joint $(\beta, b)$ dependence (Nolan, Menictas & Wand, JMLR 2020).
- **Marginal targets**: integrate over the cluster-effect distribution rather
  than plugging in fitted $b_k$ or setting $b_k = 0$.
- **Posterior importance refinement**: optional `correction = :psis` on global
  parameters after analytically integrating $b$, with operational gates
  (finite weights, $\hat{k} < 0.7$, $\mathrm{ESS} \ge 400$ from 2,000 draws,
  mean MCSE $\le 5\%$ posterior SD). Failed checks withhold requested intervals
  and covariance without silently falling back to raw VI.
